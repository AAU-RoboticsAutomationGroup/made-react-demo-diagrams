title Basic Operator Workflow 

participant Operator
participant FESTO Magazines
participant FESTO MES4
participant FESTO Line
participant QC Station
participant AMR

note over AMR: Idle at charging dock
note over FESTO Magazines: Magazines are empty before production
note over FESTO Line: System and robot cell is booted and  idle
note over QC Station: Station is empty


Operator->FESTO Magazines:Refill Cover magazines
Operator->FESTO Magazines:Refill Fuse magazines
Operator->FESTO Magazines:Refill PCB pallet
Operator->AMR: Initialize AMR
Operator->AMR: Set # of items to pick up
AMR-->Operator: AMR is initialized

Operator->Operator: Verify all system are ready

Operator->FESTO MES4:Set orders for run
FESTO MES4->FESTO Line: Apply orders to modules
FESTO Line-->FESTO MES4: Orders began production
FESTO MES4-->Operator: Orders began production

loop monitor production
loop magazines are empty
 Operator->FESTO Magazines:Refill Cover magazines
 Operator->FESTO Magazines:Refill Fuse magazines
 Operator->FESTO Magazines:Refill PCB pallet
end

AMR--> Operator: AMR arrives at QC station with products
Operator->AMR: Remove finished items from AMR
Operator->AMR: Confirm items are removed
Operator->QC Station: Move finished products to QC Station
Operator->QC Station: Perform quality control
QC Station-->Operator: Product meets QC
Operator-> Warehouse: Move finished products to warehouse

alt product does not meet QC
  QC Station-->Operator: Product does not meet QC
  Operator->FESTO Magazines: Disassemble broken products
  Operator->FESTO Magazines: Refill magazines
end

else production complete
Operator->FESTO MES4: Confirm all products are produced
FESTO MES4-->Operator: No more products to produce
Operator->AMR: Shut down AMR
Operator->FESTO Magazines: Remove all parts from magazines
Operator->FESTO Line: Shut down FESTO Line
Operator->Operator: Confirm all systems are shut down
end

note over Operator: Idle wait for new orders
